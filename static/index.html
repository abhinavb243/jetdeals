JetDeal Signals
Informational only. Not investment advice. These signals are inferred from public flight data and filings. They do not say who was on a plane or why it flew. Do your own research and follow the rules that apply to you, including insider-trading law. Contains SAMPLE (fictional) data

Open alerts

A company's jets fly to another company's HQ city more often than usual, and no deal has been announced yet.

All signals

Star a company to add it to the watchlist. Watched companies get +10 on signals they are part of. Add your own companies in data/companies.csv.

Tracked aircraft

Only aircraft registered to organizations and matched to a company in the list are tracked.

Privacy rules applied

  • Aircraft registered to individuals or co-owners are dropped. Only corporations, LLCs and partnerships are kept.
  • Only aircraft that match a company in your list are tracked. Unmatched LLCs (which may be personal holding companies) are ignored.
  • Tail numbers and ICAO addresses in data/blocklist.csv are dropped at ingest and purged on every run. Add FAA LADD entries and owner opt-out requests there.
  • PIA aircraft broadcast temporary addresses that are not in the FAA registry. Flights are requested only for registry addresses, so PIA aircraft are never linked to an owner.
  • Data comes only from public sources: OpenSky Network, the FAA Releasable Aircraft database, and SEC EDGAR.

How the score works (0–100)

  • Frequency (40): landings in the host's HQ city in the last 30 days, above the visitor's normal rate.
  • Novelty (25): how unusual the trips are compared to the 180 days before.
  • Recency (15): how recent the last landing is.
  • Day trips (10): share of stays under 12 hours, which is typical for meetings.
  • Watchlist (10): the visitor or the host is on your watchlist.
  • Leisure airports (Aspen, Sun Valley, Palm Beach) are multiplied by 0.3.
  • Confirmed = a deal between both companies followed the visits (backtest shows how many days of warning the score gave). Corroborated = a single-company filing or rumor followed. Open = nothing yet.
